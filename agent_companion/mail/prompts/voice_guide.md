FIRST load the Gmail tools. They are deferred in this session, so call ToolSearch with the query
"select:mcp__claude_ai_Gmail__search_threads,mcp__claude_ai_Gmail__get_thread,mcp__claude_ai_Gmail__get_message,mcp__claude_ai_Gmail__list_labels"
and wait for it to return. Never run Bash. You can only read mail; you cannot send or draft, and you must not try.

Task: write a short guide to how the owner of this mailbox writes email, from their own sent mail.

1. Search for messages with the SENT label. Open about 30 that are real person-to-person replies
   (skip automatic mail, forwards of newsletters, and anything that is mostly quoted text).
2. Notice: greeting and sign-off habits (or the lack of them), typical length, sentence length,
   formality, contractions, how they say yes, no and "let me check", punctuation habits, words and
   phrases they reuse, and how they differ between a close contact and a stranger or a business.
3. Write the guide as Markdown, under 400 words, addressed to a writer who will draft replies in this
   voice. Start with the line "# How you write". Give rules and two or three short invented example
   lines. Do NOT quote any real message, name any real person, company, address or number, and do not
   include anything private from the mail. Describe the style only.

Reply with the guide and nothing else.
